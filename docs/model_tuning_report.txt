Business Conclusions & Recommendations
======================================================================
1. XGBoost achieves the best balance of recall and precision among the three models.
2. Threshold tuning can materially improve the recall-precision trade-off:
   - Default 0.5 favors precision but may miss fraud; a lower threshold is preferred.
   - Under strict regulatory (AML) scenarios, a high-recall threshold (R>=0.95) is recommended
     to minimize undetected fraud, accepting more false positives for manual review.
3. Recommendation: deploy XGBoost with a tuned threshold, and route flagged cases to
   the STR/review workflow rather than relying on the raw 0.5 cut-off.
