# Risk & Compliance Analysis Dimensions

1. Customer Dimension: Comparison of delinquency rate, non‑performing rate and transaction behaviour for customers across different risk levels.

2. Transaction Dimension: Risk distribution across transaction amount ranges, transaction timestamps and transaction channels.

3. Time Dimension: Monthly / weekly trend changes of delinquency rate and violation rate.

4. Compliance Dimension: Proportion analysis of compliance violations across different business lines and product offerings.

5. Risk Identification: Identify high‑risk customer groups, high‑risk transaction scenarios and potential compliance gaps.
