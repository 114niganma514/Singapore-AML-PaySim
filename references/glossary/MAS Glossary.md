| **Term** | **Definition** |
| --- | --- |
| MAS | Monetary Authority of Singapore (central bank + financial regulator). |
| AML/CFT | Anti-Money Laundering / Countering the Financing of Terrorism. |
| MAS Notice 626 | AML/CFT notice covering CDD, monitoring, and STR reporting expectations (risk-based). |
| KYC | Know Your Customer: identity verification and onboarding checks. |
| CDD / EDD | Customer Due Diligence / Enhanced Due Diligence (e.g., for PEPs or higher risk). |
| PEP | Politically Exposed Person; higher risk requiring additional controls. |
| UBO | Ultimate Beneficial Owner; the natural person(s) who ultimately own/control a business. |
| STR | Suspicious Transaction Report; escalation/filing when suspicious activity is detected. |
| Travel Rule | Information requirements around certain cross-border transfers (conceptual demo trigger here). |
| PDPA | Personal Data Protection Act (Singapore): consent, purpose limitation, protection, and retention. |
| Structuring | Breaking transactions into smaller amounts to avoid thresholds or detection. |
| Dormant account surge | Sudden high-value activity after a long inactive period. |
